<h1>📊 Data Analysis Using Matplotlib</h1>

<p>
In data analytics, visualization helps in understanding data distribution, 
relationships, and patterns. Using Matplotlib, data analysis is commonly 
classified into <b>Univariate</b>, <b>Bivariate</b>, and <b>Multivariate</b> analysis.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>🔹 Univariate Analysis</h2>
<p>
<b>Univariate analysis</b> involves analyzing <b>a single variable</b> at a time.
The main objective is to understand the distribution, central tendency, 
and spread of the data.
</p>

<h4>📌 Key Objectives</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Understand data distribution</li>
  <li>Identify outliers</li>
  <li>Analyze frequency and range</li>
</ul>

<h4>📈 Common Visualizations</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Histogram</li>
  <li>Bar Chart</li>
  <li>Box Plot</li>
  <li>Pie Chart</li>
</ul>

<h4>🧠 Example Use Case</h4>
<p>
Analyzing the distribution of student marks, product prices, or customer ages.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>🔹 Bivariate Analysis</h2>
<p>
<b>Bivariate analysis</b> focuses on analyzing the relationship between 
<b>two variables</b>. It helps in identifying correlations, trends, and comparisons.
</p>

<h4>📌 Key Objectives</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Understand relationships between variables</li>
  <li>Detect correlation or dependency</li>
  <li>Compare numerical or categorical data</li>
</ul>

<h4>📈 Common Visualizations</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Scatter Plot</li>
  <li>Line Plot</li>
  <li>Grouped Bar Chart</li>
  <li>Box Plot (category vs numeric)</li>
</ul>

<h4>🧠 Example Use Case</h4>
<p>
Analyzing the relationship between study hours and exam scores, 
or sales versus time.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>🔹 Multivariate Analysis</h2>
<p>
<b>Multivariate analysis</b involves analyzing <b>more than two variables</b> 
simultaneously. This type of analysis helps in understanding complex 
relationships and patterns within data.
</p>

<h4>📌 Key Objectives</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Analyze interactions among multiple variables</li>
  <li>Identify patterns and clusters</li>
  <li>Support predictive modeling</li>
</ul>

<h4>📈 Common Visualizations</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Pair Plot</li>
  <li>Heatmap</li>
  <li>3D Scatter Plot</li>
  <li>Bubble Chart</li>
</ul>

<h4>🧠 Example Use Case</h4>
<p>
Analyzing customer behavior using age, income, location, and purchase amount,
or understanding multiple factors affecting business performance.
</p>

<p>
Understanding univariate, bivariate, and multivariate analysis is essential for:
</p>
